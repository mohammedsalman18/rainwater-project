Rainwater Harvesting

Discover how residential rainwater harvesting can conserve water, reduce water bills, and contribute to sustainable living in urban and rural areas. By collecting and storing rainwater, households can reduce dependency on municipal water supplies, ensuring a reliable water source during dry spells. This eco-friendly practice also helps prevent soil erosion, minimize flooding risks, and recharge groundwater levels, making it an essential step toward environmental conservation and resilience.

Methods of Rainwater Harvesting

Roof Catchment Systems

Collects and stores rainwater from roofs for household use.

Underground Storage

Stores water underground to save space and reduce evaporation.

Percolation Pits

Recharges groundwater by allowing rainwater to seep into the earth.

Benefits of Rainwater Harvesting

Water Conservation

Helps in conserving water for future generations.

Cost Savings

Reduces water bills significantly by using stored rainwater.

Sustainability

Promotes eco-friendly practices in residential areas.

Why Rainwater Harvesting?

  • Efficient water management for households.
  • Reduces dependency on municipal water supply.
  • Promotes environmental sustainability.
